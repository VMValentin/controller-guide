Walkie-talkie: a player on Reddit says RB on the original We Were Here for Xbox, but Xbox reviews of the series (We Were Here Too / Together) say LB. Check the on-screen prompt. Interact on A is confirmed by reviews. Only one person can talk at a time, like a real radio.

Co-op tips

  • Play in separate rooms (or wear headphones) so you only hear each other through the walkie. That's the fun.
  • Describe shapes and colors slowly; the listener repeats it back before pulling any lever.
  • Say "over" when done talking, then let go of the talk button.
