Co-op tips

  • Start with the single-player War Stories: no pressure, checkpoints, and you can take turns passing the controller.
  • In multiplayer, join the same squad; the kid can play Medic (revive with a gadget) while the parent leads.
  • Turn on aim assist and lower look sensitivity in Options.
