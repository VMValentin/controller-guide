Beginner tips

  • Hold the controller with thumbs on the two sticks; index fingers rest on the triggers, middle fingers can float behind.
  • Left thumb moves, right thumb looks. Practice one at a time: stand still and just look around first.
  • Lower look sensitivity in game settings. It makes the camera far less dizzying.
  • Most games show button prompts on screen. Match the letter and color (A green, B red, X blue, Y yellow).
  • Press the Xbox button for the Steam overlay; Steam Input lets you remap any button per game.
  • Pick a calm spot and do 5 minutes of drills before jumping into the action.
